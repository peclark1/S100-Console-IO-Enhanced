Console I/O V29.11 - IBM PC VGA glyph preservation fix
=========================================================

* Fixes missing horizontal/edge pixels in the IBM PC VGA font.
* Root cause: V29.10 reduced the PCFace 8x16 source glyphs to 8x12 with
  nearest-neighbor row selection. Several one-scanline strokes were skipped
  entirely (for example the top/middle strokes of B and portions of digits).
* V29.11 does not resample ordinary glyphs. It crops/shifts the occupied
  source scanlines into the 12-row terminal cell, preserving every original
  scanline for glyphs that fit. Rare taller glyphs merge rows with OR so no
  lit source pixel is discarded.
* Default and DEC VT220 font data are unchanged from V29.10.
* No VGA timing, geometry, color, scan-code, keyboard, VT100, or EEPROM
  behavior was intentionally changed.
